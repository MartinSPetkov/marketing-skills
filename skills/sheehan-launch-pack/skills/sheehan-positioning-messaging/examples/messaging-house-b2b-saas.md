# Example messaging house: Acme Insights (B2B SaaS product analytics)

Full messaging house for the hypothetical Acme Insights launch introduced in the GTM plan example.

## Positioning statement

For Heads of Product at Series B-D B2B SaaS companies who need to see how users move through their product but don't have a dedicated data team, Acme Insights is a product analytics tool that installs in 30 minutes and answers the 5 questions every PM asks on day one. Unlike Mixpanel and Amplitude, our product is priced for teams under 50 and doesn't require a data engineer to maintain.

## Tagline

Product analytics your PM team can actually run.

## Pillar 1: Up and running in 30 minutes

**Subhead:** Install our SDK, tag your first events, and see funnels by lunchtime. No data engineer required.

**Proof:**

- One-line JavaScript SDK; React, Swift, and Android variants.
- 18 pre-built dashboards that cover the questions PMs ask on day one (activation, retention, feature adoption, funnel drop-off).
- Customer quote: "I set it up between two meetings and had a usable funnel by the end of the day." (VP Product, Latitude)
- Compare: average Mixpanel onboarding time in G2 reviews is 2-3 weeks.

## Pillar 2: Answers, not a query language

**Subhead:** Ask questions in plain English. Get charts in seconds. No SQL, no data team required.

**Proof:**

- Natural-language query interface: "Show me activated users by week for last quarter."
- Auto-generated cohorts based on behavior.
- Customer quote: "We stopped waiting for data requests. Product just gets the answer." (Director of Product, Wavelength)
- Compare: Amplitude Charts requires an event taxonomy most teams don't have time to build.

## Pillar 3: Priced for teams, not enterprises

**Subhead:** Flat monthly fee based on team size, not on data volume. Predictable and fair.

**Proof:**

- $499/month for teams under 50.
- Unlimited events, unlimited seats.
- No surprise overage charges. Compare to event-based pricing at Mixpanel and Amplitude, which doubles many mid-market bills mid-year.
- Customer quote: "Finance approved us in one email because the price doesn't move." (CFO, Nimbus)

## Persona variants

### Primary persona: Head of Product at Series B-D SaaS

**Opening pain:** "I want to know what users are doing, but setting up Mixpanel means a 3-month data project."
**Lead message:** Pillar 1 (speed to value).
**Proof priority:** 30-minute install; day-one dashboards; customer quote from peer CPO.

### Secondary persona: PM without analytics today

**Opening pain:** "I don't even know where to start. My dashboards are the ones engineering threw together two years ago."
**Lead message:** Pillar 2 (answers, not a query language).
**Proof priority:** Natural-language interface; pre-built dashboards; no SQL needed.

### Tertiary persona: Finance approver at the buying company

**Opening pain:** "Our analytics bill doubled last year because nobody watched the event volume."
**Lead message:** Pillar 3 (predictable pricing).
**Proof priority:** Flat price, no overages; peer CFO quote.

## Language notes

- **Use:** "product analytics" (not "user analytics" or "behavioral analytics"), "set up in 30 minutes" (specific, not "fast"), "your PM team can actually run" (implies the alternative requires other teams).
- **Avoid:** "single source of truth" (buzzword), "unlock insights" (generic), "empower your team" (empty).

## Competitive watch-outs

- **Don't claim:** "More powerful than Amplitude." Amplitude has features we don't. Lead with simplicity and price instead.
- **Own:** "Priced for mid-market, built for PMs." Direct competitors aren't.
- **Neutralize:** Objection "but we might outgrow you." Response: "Our biggest customer is at 700 employees; when you cross that, we help you hand off to Amplitude cleanly."
