# Tier 1 backward schedule

Week by week from T-12 to T-0. Adjust dates to your launch; the sequence holds.

## T-12 weeks: Kickoff

- Launch kickoff meeting with full team.
- GTM plan draft complete.
- Launch tier and goals signed off by exec team.
- Launch lead named.
- RACI built.
- Slack channels and shared drive set up.
- First monthly exec update.

## T-11 weeks: Research and inputs

- Target market research in flight (surveys, interviews).
- Competitive matrix built.
- PR agency briefed (or internal comms plan stood up).

## T-10 weeks: Target and positioning locked

- Target market and personas final.
- Messaging workshop scheduled.
- Analyst relations briefing plan started (Gartner, Forrester, IDC).
- Pricing work with Finance kicks off.

## T-9 weeks: Messaging drafting

- Messaging house first draft.
- Positioning statement draft.
- Press release outline.

## T-8 weeks: Messaging signed off

- Messaging house final.
- Positioning statement signed off by exec sponsor.
- Sales roadshow schedule set.
- Content calendar locked (blog, social, email).
- Second monthly exec update.

## T-7 weeks: Asset drafting

- Press release draft.
- Launch blog post draft.
- Sales pitch script draft.
- Homepage and product page designs.

## T-6 weeks: Press plan in flight

- Press list built.
- PR agency runs exclusive-pitch conversations if applicable.
- Analyst briefings scheduled for 4-5 weeks out.
- Sales comm doc / FAQ final.

## T-5 weeks: Analyst briefings begin

- Analyst briefings happen over 2 weeks (T-5 to T-4).
- One sheet, deck, ROI calculator final.
- Paid campaigns built and ready to schedule.
- Third monthly exec update.

## T-4 weeks: Sales training

- Sales training session (live).
- Certification rubric shared.
- In-product announcement built and tested.
- External email list pulled and segmented.
- Customer case study interviews wrapped up.

## T-3 weeks: Rehearsal prep

- All external assets in final review.
- **Messaging no-change date passed.** No changes without exec sign-off.
- Sales reps recording certification pitches.
- CS outreach plan finalized.

## T-2 weeks: Certification and launch materials final

- **90%+ of sales reps certified.**
- All launch materials (blog, homepage, emails, social) scheduled.
- Press embargo set with reporters.
- Day-of runbook written and shared.

## T-1 week: Final prep and go/no-go

- Final exec update.
- 5-minute all-hands preview.
- **Go/no-go meeting.** 30 minutes with core team and exec sponsor.
- All-hands preview.
- Homepage and blog in staging, reviewed end to end.
- Dress rehearsal (T-5 days): walk the launch day checklist live, with all core team on the call.

## T-0: Launch day

- See `launch-day-checklist.md`.

## T+1 to T+7: First week

- Daily Slack snapshot.
- Sales team daily check-in.
- Support ticket trend monitoring.
- Day 7 written update to company.

## T+8 to T+30: First month

- Rolling thunder content.
- Customer case studies publishing.
- Day-30 full report.
- All-hands metrics share.

## T+30 to T+90: Mid and final scorecard

- Day 60 trend read.
- Day 90 final scorecard.
- Post-launch debrief.
- Debrief findings shared with exec team and launch team.

## Adjusting for Tier 2

Compress to 6 weeks. Drop analyst briefings, sales certification, dress rehearsal, PR agency. Keep: GTM plan, messaging house, sales training, blog/homepage update, customer email, in-product announcement, day-7 and day-30 reports.

## Adjusting for Tier 3

Compress to 2 weeks. Keep: one-page plan, blog post or changelog entry, internal Slack announcement, day-7 metric snapshot. Drop everything else.
