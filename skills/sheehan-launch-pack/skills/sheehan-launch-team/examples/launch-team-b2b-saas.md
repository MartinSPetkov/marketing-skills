# Example: Tier 1 launch team for a mid-market B2B SaaS

Launch: Acme Insights, a new paid product analytics tier. 90 days out.

## Launch lead

Jane, Sr PMM. Accountable for the launch overall.

## Core team (weekly sync attendees)

- Jane (PMM) - launch lead
- Raj (PM) - product readiness
- Maria (VP Sales) - sales readiness
- Sam (Director CS) - customer readiness
- Dev (Content lead) - external content
- Priya (Growth lead) - paid and lifecycle
- Lily (Design lead) - creative
- Tom (PR, external agency) - press

## Executive sponsor

VP Product. Signs off at go/no-go. Sits in on monthly updates.

## RACI highlights

- GTM plan: R+A Jane, C Raj/Maria/Sam, I exec team.
- Positioning and messaging: R Jane, A CMO, C Raj/Maria/CEO.
- Press: R Tom, A Jane, C CMO/CEO.
- Sales certification: R Maria, A Maria, C Jane.
- In-product announcement: R Raj, A Raj, C Jane/Lily.
- Launch day internal email: R+A Jane, I all hands.

See `references/raci-template.md` for the full grid.

## Meeting cadence

- Launch kickoff: May 1 at all-hands, 5 minutes. Swag: "Acme Insights" notebooks.
- Weekly sync: Tuesdays 10-10:30am PT. PMM chairs.
- Monthly exec update: first Thursday of exec staff. Jane, 15 min.
- Sales roadshow: 4 sessions across regions, Jun 1-20.
- All-hands preview: Jun 18, 5 minutes.
- Launch day: Jun 26. Internal email, swag drop, launch emoji.
- Debrief: Jul 10, 90 minutes with core team.

## Internal comms plan

- Slack channels: #launch-insights (whole company), #launch-insights-core (core team).
- Biweekly written update every other Friday, 200 words max.
- Exec update: first Thursday each month.
- Sales roadshow: 4 regional sessions.
- Launch week: 5-min all-hands preview on Jun 25; internal email + swag Jun 26; metrics share at Jul 10 all-hands.

## Sales enablement

- Training: Jun 15, 90 min, live and recorded.
- Certification: 90%+ of reps certified by Jun 22.
- Assets: FAQ, pitch script, one sheet, deck update, 3 battle cards, ROI calculator.
- Day-1 Slack channel: #launch-insights-sales, open Jun 26 through Jul 10.

## CS enablement

- Training: Jun 18, 30 min.
- Top 50 accounts flagged for proactive outreach.
- Email templates ready Jun 20.
- Sam reports back on outreach outcomes at each weekly sync.

## Post-launch

- Day-30 metrics post to all-hands: Jul 26.
- Debrief: Jul 10.
- Debrief findings shared with exec team: Jul 17.
