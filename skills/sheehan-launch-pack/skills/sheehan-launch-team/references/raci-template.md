# RACI template for a B2B SaaS launch

One row per major deliverable. One Accountable per row; never two. Everyone else is Responsible, Consulted, or Informed.

## Section 1: Strategic readiness

| Deliverable | R | A | C | I |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GTM plan | PMM | PMM | PM, Sales lead, CS lead, CMO | Exec team |
| Target market and personas | PMM | PMM | PM, Sales, CS | Exec team |
| Positioning and messaging house | PMM | CMO | PMM, PM, Sales, CEO | All hands |
| Competitive matrix | PMM | PMM | PM, Sales | Launch team |
| Pricing | Pricing lead | CFO | PMM, Sales, CEO | Launch team |
| KPIs and goals | PMM | CMO | CRO, VP Product | Exec team |

## Rules

- **One A per row.** If two people think they're accountable, the work splits badly. Pick.
- **R and A can be the same person.** That's fine for smaller launches.
- **Consulted means before, Informed means after.** Don't flip them.
- **Keep the list to 25-40 rows.** More than that and the RACI itself becomes overhead.
- **Revisit at each weekly sync.** If ownership is drifting, update the RACI.

## When to flex

For Tier 2 launches, collapse roles. PMM might be Accountable for most rows. Design and Content might be a single person. That's fine; the principle is clarity, not bureaucracy.

For Tier 3, a RACI is overkill. Name an owner and a sign-off person. Move on.
