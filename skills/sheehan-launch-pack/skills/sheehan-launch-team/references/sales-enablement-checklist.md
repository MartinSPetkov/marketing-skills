# Sales enablement checklist

The sales team is the launch's biggest amplifier on Tier 1 and Tier 2. These are the assets, moments, and checks that make reps ready to pitch.

## The minimum asset pack

Every launch, regardless of tier, ships with:

1. **Sales comm doc / internal FAQ.** 1-3 pages. Covers what's launching, why, who it's for, pricing, how to position against alternatives, objection handling, where to go with questions. This is the single source of truth. If a rep asks a question not covered here, add it.

2. **Pitch script / talking points.** 2-3 minute verbal pitch. Problem, solution, proof, price, next step.

3. **One sheet (PDF).** Leave-behind for prospects. Value prop at top, 3-4 benefits with proof, pricing or contact CTA at bottom.

4. **Sales deck slides.** 3-5 slides added to the master pitch deck. Problem slide, solution slide, product shot, customer proof, pricing.

For Tier 1, add:

5. **Competitive battle cards.** One per major competitor. How to win, how to lose, common traps.

6. **ROI calculator or model.** Spreadsheet or simple tool reps can share to justify the price.

7. **Demo script.** Step-by-step demo flow with talk track.

## Training

- Tier 1: Live training, 60-90 minutes. Record it. Homework: reps read the FAQ and pitch script before the session.
- Tier 2: 30-minute training or recorded walkthrough.
- Tier 3: Slack post with link to the FAQ.

## Certification (Tier 1 only)

Certification is how you know reps can actually pitch. Not a vanity exercise.

Process:

1. Every rep records a 3-minute pitch on video (Loom, Gong, Chorus).
2. Sales manager or PMM grades against a rubric: problem stated, value prop clear, proof included, price mentioned, ask made, time under 3 minutes.
3. Pass = certified, name on the list. Fail = rerecord with feedback.
4. No pitch, no lead assignment. Enforce it or it becomes theater.

Target: 90%+ of reps certified by T-2 weeks.

## The day-1 Slack channel

Set up `#launch-[name]-sales` the morning of launch. Purpose: reps can ask live questions, PMM answers within an hour during business hours. Close after 2 weeks.

## CS enablement

Customer Success runs a parallel flow:

1. Existing customer talking points (1 page).
2. Email templates for proactive outreach.
3. Segmented account list: who to reach, who to wait on.
4. CS training session (30 min for Tier 1, Slack post for Tier 2).

## Signs enablement worked

- Reps pitch consistently (content, not word-for-word).
- Sales asks fewer questions in the first 2 weeks than the last launch.
- Deals mention the new product in week 1.
- CS outreach yields measurable upgrades or expansion in 30-60 days.

## Signs enablement failed

- Reps send customers to marketing for basic questions.
- Pitches vary wildly in content and quality.
- Deals stall because reps can't answer pricing or competitive questions.
- CS doesn't mention the launch on customer calls.

When it fails, don't blame sales. Fix the FAQ, retrain, and certify.
