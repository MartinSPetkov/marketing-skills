# One-page persona template

Keep the persona to one page. If it grows past that, the team won't use it.

## Template

```
# Persona: [Name]

**Role:** [Title, seniority]
**Company:** [Size, stage, industry]
**Geography:** [Region]

## Job to be done
**Functional job:** What they're literally trying to accomplish. "Ship a weekly report to execs on time without errors."
**Emotional job:** How they want to feel. "Not look sloppy in front of the CFO."
**Social job:** How they want to be seen. "The one who has it together."

## Pain points (in their words)
- "[Exact quote from interview.]"
- "[Exact quote from interview.]"
- "[Exact quote from interview.]"

## Consideration set
- [Alternative 1]: Why they looked at it. Why they ruled it out.
- [Alternative 2]: ...
- [Alternative 3]: ...
- Status quo / doing nothing: [What that looks like today.]

## Buying process
- Who uses it: [Role]
- Who approves: [Role]
- Who signs: [Role]
- Budget source: [Department]
- Procurement speed: [Fast / slow / painful]
- Typical budget range: [$X-$Y]

## Channels (where they learn)
- [Publication / podcast / community]
- [Publication / podcast / community]
- [Peer source: Slack group, conference, analyst]

## Must-haves
- [Feature or capability]
- [Feature or capability]

## Nice-to-haves
- [Feature or capability]

## What they are NOT
- Not [adjacent role].
- Not [adjacent company type].
- Not [segment that looks similar but behaves differently].
```

## Rules

- **Use real quotes.** Invented ones smell invented. If you don't have a quote, leave it blank and run more interviews.
- **Name the alternatives.** "Other tools" is useless. "Mixpanel, Amplitude, or a homegrown SQL dashboard" is useful.
- **Show the buying process.** If the user and the buyer are different roles, both must be in the persona.
- **Keep the NOT list.** It forces discipline.

## Common mistakes

- Demographics without behavior. "35-45, college educated" tells you nothing about how they buy.
- One persona covering multiple segments. Split them.
- Persona covers the buyer, not the user (or vice versa) when both matter. Write two.
- Stale persona. Refresh every 12-18 months or after a major product or market shift.
