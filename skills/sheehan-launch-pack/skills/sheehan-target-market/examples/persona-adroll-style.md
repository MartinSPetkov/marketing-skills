# Example persona: "The Growth Marketer at a Mid-Market DTC Brand"

A worked persona synthesized from 22 JTBD interviews for a hypothetical retargeting product launch. This is the format and level of specificity to aim for.

## Persona: Nadia, the Growth Marketer

**Role:** Head of Growth or Senior Growth Marketer
**Company:** Direct-to-consumer brand, $10M-$100M revenue, 20-100 employees
**Geography:** US, primarily East Coast and California

## Job to be done

**Functional job:** Bring back shoppers who abandon the cart or browse without buying, and prove which channel drove the conversion.
**Emotional job:** Not feel like she's wasting ad spend. Know when to cut a campaign.
**Social job:** Be seen by the CEO as the person who can actually move revenue, not just "run ads."

## Pain points (in their words)

- "I can't tell if Meta retargeting is still working since iOS 14. I just see the cost going up and I'm guessing."
- "My attribution looks great in the ad platform and terrible in Shopify. I don't know which one to trust."
- "I need a dashboard I can share with the CEO that doesn't take me two hours to build every Monday."

## Consideration set

- AdRoll: Used before at a previous job. Felt dated but it worked.
- Meta + Google native retargeting: Current setup. Losing confidence since iOS 14.
- Klaviyo: Uses it for email but not for display. Wondered if it was enough.
- Hiring an agency: Quoted $15K/month. Too expensive for this stage.
- Doing nothing: What she's been doing for 3 months. Revenue flat.

## Buying process

- Who uses it: Growth Marketer (Nadia).
- Who approves: CEO or Head of Marketing.
- Who signs: CEO at this stage.
- Budget source: Marketing budget, paid media line.
- Procurement speed: Fast. 1-2 weeks from demo to signed contract.
- Typical budget range: $1K-$5K/month in software, plus ad spend.

## Channels (where they learn)

- Podcasts: Shopify Masters, Marketing Against the Grain, DTC Newsletter.
- Slack: DTC community, Growth Hackers.
- Twitter: Follows 20-30 growth marketers and founders.
- Conferences: Shoptalk, DTC Day. Goes to 1-2 per year.

## Must-haves

- Works with iOS 14+ privacy restrictions.
- Ties directly into Shopify for real conversion data.
- Dashboards she can screenshot and send to the CEO.

## Nice-to-haves

- Email + display in one tool.
- AI recommendations for audience segments.

## What she is NOT

- Not an enterprise marketer with a team of 10. She runs growth alone or with a contractor.
- Not a brand marketer focused on awareness. She is measured on CAC and ROAS.
- Not a founder wearing the marketing hat part-time. This is her full-time job and her expertise.

## Implications for launch

- Channels: DTC podcasts, Slack communities, Twitter. Not LinkedIn ads.
- Messaging: Lead with iOS 14 survival, attribution clarity, CEO-ready dashboards. Not "holistic growth platform."
- Sales process: Self-serve trial plus a 15-minute demo option. No 6-call sales cycle.
- Size check: Estimated 15,000 US brands in this band. Enough for a $20M ARR business at 1% penetration.
