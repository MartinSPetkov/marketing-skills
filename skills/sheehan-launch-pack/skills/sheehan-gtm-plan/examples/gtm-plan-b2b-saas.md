# Example GTM plan: Acme Insights (B2B SaaS, Tier 1)

This is a worked example of the full Sheehan GTM plan for a hypothetical analytics product launching at a mid-market SaaS company.

## Launch go/no-go

1. Adoption, usage, or revenue goals? Yes. 500 paying accounts in 90 days.
2. Customers asking for it? Yes. 42 customers in the roadmap tracker.
3. Game-changer for the industry? No.
4. Purchasable, not a free update? Yes. New paid add-on at $499/month.
5. Improves competitive position? Yes. Closes the gap against Mixpanel.

Four yes. Launch is warranted.

## Key Info

- **Who (target market):** Heads of Product at Series B-D B2B SaaS companies (50-500 employees) who already have a BI tool but no product analytics. NOT for: solo founders, consumer apps, enterprise accounts with a dedicated data team.
- **Who (internal team):** Exec sponsor: VP Product. Launch lead: Sr PMM (Jane). PM partner: Raj. Sales lead: Maria. Content lead: Dev. PR: external agency (Warm Room).
- **What (product stage):** Closed beta for 60 days, then GA at launch.
- **When (launch milestones):** Beta close Jun 1. Sales certification complete Jun 15. Analyst briefings Jun 18-22. Press embargo Jun 24. GA launch Jun 26.
- **Where (top channels):** Press, homepage, product page, external email to prospect list, paid digital (LinkedIn + Google), in-product announcement to existing customers, partner co-marketing with Segment.
- **Why (goals):** 500 paying accounts in 90 days. $2.5M new ARR in 6 months. 12 press mentions in Tier 1 trade pubs in launch month. 40% of existing customer base upgrades to the new tier within 6 months.

## Tier

Tier 1. Category extension, new pricing tier, sales needs certification, PR push, full rolling thunder.

## Bundle decision

Standalone. Adjacent AI features (auto-insights, anomaly detection) ship 6 weeks later and will get their own Tier 2 bundled launch.
