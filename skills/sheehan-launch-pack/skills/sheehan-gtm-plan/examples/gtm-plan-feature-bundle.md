# Example GTM plan: "Speed Pack" feature bundle (Tier 2)

A worked example of a bundled launch. Three small features (query cache, parallel exports, bulk API) ship together under a "Speed Pack" story. Individually they'd be Tier 3 changelog entries. Bundled, they clear the bar for Tier 2.

## Launch go/no-go

1. Adoption or revenue goals? Yes. Retention lift target (reduce 6-month churn by 1.5 pts).
2. Customers asking? Yes. Top 3 support complaints all touch speed.
3. Game-changer? No.
4. Purchasable? No, included in existing plans.
5. Competitive position? Yes. Closes speed gap vs. Looker.

Three yes, one no. Launch is warranted as Tier 2.

## Key Info

- **Who (target market):** Existing data analysts and BI leads in our customer base who run 10+ queries per day. NOT for: new prospects (this launch is retention, not acquisition).
- **Who (internal team):** Launch lead: PMM (Priya). PM partner: Ben. CS lead: Sam.
- **What (product stage):** GA for all three features simultaneously.
- **When:** One milestone: Aug 14 launch.
- **Where:** Blog post, customer email, in-product announcement, CS outreach to top accounts, release notes. No press, no paid.
- **Why (goals):** 1.5 pt reduction in 6-month churn. 60% of active customers use at least one of the three features within 30 days.

## Tier

Tier 2. Existing customer focus. No PR. Single blog + email + in-product.

## Bundle decision

Bundled as "Speed Pack." Theme: faster queries, faster exports, faster integrations. Narrative ties the three together and makes the release feel substantial.
