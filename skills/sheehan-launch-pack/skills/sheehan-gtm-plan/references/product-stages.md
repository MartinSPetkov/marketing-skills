# Product stages

Knowing what stage a product is in tells you what kind of launch is appropriate. Don't talk publicly about a product before beta. Use this table to set expectations with product managers and set goals for each stage.

## Market validation ("homework" stage)

**Goals:** establish a hypothesis of product/market fit, including target market. Understand if the product is worth building. Understand the competitive landscape.

**Signals you're ready for the next stage:** decent chance of product/market fit based on prospect interviews and competitive review.

**Launch activities:** deep customer interviews, competitive review. No external marketing.

## Alpha (evaluation)

**Goals:** work closely with under ten customers. Validate the concept. Iterate to MVP. Be willing to kill the product here.

**Signals you're ready for the next stage:** a handful of live, happy customers. Commercialization planning started with product marketing. International markets evaluated.

**Launch activities:** start the GTM plan. Draft initial messaging and positioning. Set early launch goals. Develop target persona drafts. Produce bare-bones launch content: sales comm doc, product one sheet, deck.

## Closed beta (testing with customers)

**Goals:** drive early adoption. Start selling into international if applicable. Iterate product to fit market. Test marketing messaging. Create KPIs for moving to open beta or GA.

**Signals you're ready for the next stage:** specific KPIs achieved. Customers live across all target verticals. Comprehensive testing done. Pricing finalized. GTM plan finalized.

**Launch activities:** finalize GTM plan. Develop and test pricing. Refine personas. Test messaging on website. Build out product page, demo videos, educational content like webinars. Start recruiting customer testimonials.

## Open beta (testing with customers through sales)

**Goals:** sales can sell at their discretion. All marketing materials localized. Self-serve UI tested on a percentage of customers. Potential marketing push.

**Signals you're ready for the next stage:** KPIs maintained. Self-serve UI ready to go live globally. Product works at scale. GTM plan kicked off.

**Launch activities:** heavy enablement. All marketing assets ready. Regional translations done.

## GA (general availability)

**Goals:** product is available for all customers.

**Launch activities:** the product "launch" date happens. PMM runs the day-of-launch calendar and coordinates PR, events, product, and sales.

## The beta escape hatch

If the product manager is nervous about a launch date because the product isn't perfect, suggest launching as beta instead of GA. Beta buys time for iteration while still generating awareness. Gmail stayed in beta for five years.
