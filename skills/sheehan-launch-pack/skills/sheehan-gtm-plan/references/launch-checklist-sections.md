# Launch checklist: the four sections

The checklist is a shared spreadsheet (Sheehan uses Google Sheets) with one tab for each section. Each row has: task, owner, due date, status, priority, notes, links. Update weekly. Share with every launch communication.

## Section 1: Strategic Readiness

Everything in the Key Info tab, restated as checklist rows with owners and due dates.

- Target market defined (specifically, including who it is NOT for).
- Personas drafted or updated.
- Competitive landscape reviewed.
- Positioning and messaging house approved by execs.
- Internal stakeholder list locked.
- Product scope confirmed (features, stage, specs).
- Launch milestones set.
- Channel plan approved.
- KPIs and goals approved.

Nothing downstream starts until Section 1 is green.

## Section 2: External Promotion

Rows for each external channel the launch will use. Pull from the external channels reference. Typical rows:

- Press release drafted, reviewed, scheduled.
- PR agency briefed, pitch list built, embargo plan set.
- Homepage hero updated.
- Product page rewritten.
- Blog post drafted and scheduled.
- Email copy drafted, segment list pulled, send scheduled.
- Social posts drafted and queued.
- Paid digital creative built, campaigns configured.
- Case study or customer quote captured.
- In-product announcement configured.
- Help center articles updated.

## Section 3: Sales Enablement

Rows for each sales asset and enablement moment. Pull from the sales enablement reference. Typical rows:

- Sales comm doc / internal FAQ drafted.
- Pitch script and talking points drafted.
- One sheet designed, reviewed, uploaded.
- Slides drafted and added to master deck.
- Sales training session scheduled and delivered.
- Certification rubric built (Tier 1 only).
- Assets uploaded to the sales drive or intranet.
- KPIs and incentives set with sales leadership.

## Section 4: Internal Launch Communications

Rows for each internal moment, organized by time leading up to launch.

- Launch kickoff announcement to the company.
- Monthly exec update (with deck) in the exec staff meeting.
- Biweekly launch team syncs set up.
- Roadshow deck presented to sales, marketing, product, regional teams.
- Slack channels created (steering committee + broader).
- All-hands slot booked with a 3-5 minute update.
- Launch day internal email scheduled.
- Swag, quiz, or rally moment planned.

Not optional. See the `sheehan-launch-team` skill for the full internal comms playbook.
