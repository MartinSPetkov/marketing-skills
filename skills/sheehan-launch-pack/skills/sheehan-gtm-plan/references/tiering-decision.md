# Tiering decision tree

Use this to pick a tier. If a launch meets the criteria for multiple tiers, pick the higher one, but fight the urge to inflate.

## Tier 1: The flagship

A Tier 1 is the biggest launch of the quarter. One to two per quarter, ever. Signals:

- New product category or major expansion.
- Changes the company's sales story or pricing.
- Priced to drive real revenue impact this year.
- Warrants press coverage and an event.
- Sales team needs certification before they can sell it.
- Exec sponsor expects to talk about it on the next earnings or all-hands.

Typical assets: press release and PR agency push, customer event or conference tie-in, launch video, homepage takeover, sales certification, one sheet, customer case study at launch, paid digital, rolling thunder plan for 90 days after.

## Tier 2: Important but not category-changing

Tier 2 launches serve core customers and sales motions but don't redefine the market. You'll have several per quarter. Signals:

- New feature that unlocks a use case or expands existing product.
- Tied to a persona sales is already selling to.
- Needs sales enablement but not certification.
- Warrants a blog post and email but not a press push.

Typical assets: blog, external email, in-product messaging, updated sales slides, internal comm doc, customer-facing help center update, social posts.

## Tier 3: Cosmetic or maintenance

Tier 3 launches are UI tweaks, bug fixes, under-the-hood work, or minor quality-of-life changes. Dozens per quarter is normal. Signals:

- No new pricing or packaging implication.
- No new persona.
- Customers will notice in the product but won't change behavior.

Typical assets: changelog entry, help center note, optional in-product toast. No external promotion.

## The effort trap

Tier is not a function of engineering effort. A year-long back-end rewrite can be a Tier 3 launch if the customer doesn't see it. Thank the engineers at an all-hands. Don't burn market attention on invisible work.

## The fatigue test

If every launch this quarter is Tier 1, the market and sales team will tune out. Pick one or two flagships per quarter. Put the rest on a lower tier.
