# Sales enablement assets

Sales and customer-facing teams are the front line. If they don't know about a launch or can't speak credibly about it, the launch fails no matter how good the external promotion is.

Ideally every asset below is ready before launch date.

| Asset | When to use | Time to produce | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| All-hands announcement | Weekly all-hands is a fast way to put the launch on sales' radar. | Low | 1 |
| Goals and incentives | Work with sales leadership on KPIs and incentives specific to this product. | Medium | 1 |
| One sheet | High-quality PDF for prospects and follow-ups. Takes longer than expected (design, screenshots, messaging). | Medium | 1 |
| Sales certification | For complex products or new positioning. Pitch script + rubric; reps must pass before they can sell. | High | 1 |
| Talking points or pitch script | Useful for complicated products where reps need structured language. | Medium | 1 |
| Asset management posting | Wherever sales already looks (intranet, Google Drive, Dropbox). | Low | 1, 2 |
| Sales training | Required so sellers understand the what and the why. Without it, reps invent their own pitch. | Medium-High | 1, 2 |
| Slides | Quick way to give sales the basics and let them bolt onto the existing deck. | Low-Medium | 1, 2 |
| Comm doc / internal FAQ | The single source of truth. Co-own with product manager. Always current. | Low-Medium | 1, 2, 3 |

## Timing rule

Enablement before launch, not on launch day. If sales first hears about a product when a customer asks them about it, you lost.

## Internal evangelism

Sales teams who love a product sell it harder. Make the launch exciting internally. Swag, quizzes, exec cameos, celebratory events for the sales team all pay back in external selling.
