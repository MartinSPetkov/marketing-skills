# Example Editorial Calendar

**Brand:** Northside Ceramics Studio  
**Primary channel:** Weekly email newsletter ("The Kiln Notes")  
**Frequency:** Every Tuesday morning  
**Planning period:** May – June 2024

---

## Context

Mission statement: Northside Ceramics Studio delivers weekly studio practice guides and behind-the-scenes teaching stories for working ceramic artists who want to deepen their craft without leaving their day jobs.

Persona: Maya, 34, freelance graphic designer, 3 years into ceramics as a serious practice. Subscribes to 4 craft newsletters. Reads in the morning before her design day starts. Trusts first-person accounts over tutorials.

Business goal: Grow email list to 500 subscribers to support a paid workshop launch in September.

---

## Content themes for May–June

Two monthly themes, each building toward the September workshop launch:

- **May: The practice itself** — how studio time works when life is also happening
- **June: The moment of stuck** — what to do when work isn't coming out right

Each month has a secondary thread toward the subscription funnel: showing the behind-the-scenes reality of preparing for a workshop.

## Production workflow

**Who does what:**

- Content brief: Studio owner (Aditi) writes a 3-bullet brief every Friday for the following Tuesday's issue. No full outline — just the angle, the specific story, and one thing readers should take away.
- First draft: Aditi drafts on Saturday morning (90 minutes max). If it runs over 90 minutes, the topic is too broad and she cuts it.
- Review: No external review — Aditi reads aloud once on Sunday to catch awkward phrasing.
- Publishing and distribution: Scheduled through ConvertKit Sunday night, sends Tuesday 7am.

**Lead time rule:** Aditi plans one month ahead. She writes one week ahead. The May calendar was finalized April 28.

**Buffer content:** Two complete issues are held in reserve at all times. Current reserve: "When your glaze doesn't match the swatch" (general troubleshooting) and "Five things I'd tell myself in year one" (general reflection). Both are perennial — no date references.

## Editorial guidelines summary

**Frequency:** Weekly, every Tuesday at 7am

**Primary channel:** Email newsletter (ConvertKit). Secondary: teaser posts on Instagram 2–3x per month linking to past issues.

**Content themes:** Two-month rotating themes tied to Maya's studio reality. Each month ends with a piece that connects to the workshop waitlist.

**Voice rules:** First person, past tense for stories, present tense for advice. No lists in body copy — Maya reads essays, not tips roundups. Maximum 600 words per issue.

**Production workflow:** Brief on Friday, draft Saturday, publish Tuesday. All content written by Aditi. Buffer of 2 complete issues maintained at all times.

**Idea capture:** Notion page "Kiln Notes ideas." Any idea gets added with engagement stage and date. Reviewed monthly when planning the next theme.

**Success metric:** Open rate above 40%, list growth of 20 new subscribers per month, 50 waitlist signups for the September workshop by August 1.
