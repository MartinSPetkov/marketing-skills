# Example audience development funnel plan

**Brand:** SeedRound.io (newsletter for pre-seed founders)  
**Current state:** 1,200 email subscribers, 38% open rate, 4.2% click rate. No community platform. A few readers reply occasionally. One reader has referred 3 friends.  
**Superfan target:** 30 founders who actively advocate for the newsletter

---

## Conversion math (working backward)

- Target: 30 superfans
- Superfans are roughly the top 2–3% of active community members
- Need approximately 1,000–1,500 active community members to produce 30 superfans
- Active community members are roughly 15–20% of engaged subscribers
- Need approximately 6,000–8,000 subscribers with healthy engagement to produce 1,000 active community members
- Current gap: 4,800–6,800 more subscribers needed

This is an 18–24 month program, not a 90-day sprint.

---

## Stage 1: Stranger to reader

**Current state:** Organic search drives ~35% of traffic. Guest content and referrals account for ~15%. Social (LinkedIn) accounts for ~50% but produces the lowest subscriber conversion rate.

**Actions:**
- Write 3 cornerstone articles targeting specific search queries from pre-seed founders (examples: "equity split co-founder agreement template," "first 90 days after pre-seed close," "how to evaluate a term sheet first-time founder"). These compound over time in a way LinkedIn posts don't.
- Pitch 2 guest appearances on podcasts the persona already listens to (specific targets: Indie Hackers podcast, The Founder Files, Founder Chats). Guest appearances convert better than social posts because the listener has already allocated 30+ minutes of attention.
- Stop producing LinkedIn posts as a primary distribution channel. Reduce to 1 LinkedIn post per week that teases the newsletter — not a standalone content piece.

**Metric to track:** Monthly unique visitors from organic search (Google Search Console). Target: 3,000/month within 6 months (currently ~900/month).

---

## Stage 2: Reader to subscriber

**Current state:** Estimated 0.8% conversion rate from unique visitor to subscriber (900 visitors/month, gaining roughly 7 new organic subscribers per month from the blog). Email opt-in is in the header and footer. No content upgrades.

**Actions:**
- Build one content upgrade tied to the highest-traffic article. If the top article is "how to evaluate a term sheet," the upgrade is a one-page term sheet red-flag checklist as a PDF. The opt-in CTA goes immediately after the relevant section in the article body.
- Rewrite the opt-in prompt. Current prompt: "Subscribe to SeedRound.io for weekly startup insights." Revised prompt: "Every Tuesday: one thing first-time founders wish they'd known in month 6 — written by founders who just passed the two-year mark. No frameworks. No VC opinion. Real numbers."
- Add a specific referral link to every newsletter footer so existing subscribers can share with a trackable URL.

**Metric to track:** Email list growth rate (net new subscribers ÷ starting list size, monthly). Target: 5% monthly growth rate within 6 months (currently estimated at ~0.6%).

---

## Stage 3: Subscriber to community member

**Current state:** 38% open rate is healthy. 4.2% click rate is moderate. ~8 replies per issue (0.7% reply rate), which is above average but indicates there's room to prompt more. No recurring reply mechanism.

**Actions:**
- End every Tuesday issue with one specific, low-friction question. Not "what do you think?" — something like: "What's the one question you're most afraid to ask your investors right now? Reply and I'll cover the most common answer in next week's issue."
- Publish a "Reader replies" section once per month featuring 3–5 responses to the previous question, with founder names (if permitted) or roles. This rewards people for replying and shows others it's worth doing.
- Email the 12 most engaged subscribers (identified by open rate + click history + prior replies) a personal note saying the newsletter has helped you write better and you'd value their honest feedback. Three questions, no more. This creates a small group of invested readers before the audience is big enough for a community platform.

**Metric to track:** Reply rate per issue. Target: 2% reply rate within 6 months (currently ~0.7%).

---

## Stage 4: Community member to superfan

**Current state:** One identified superfan (the subscriber who referred 3 friends). A handful of consistent openers who have replied at least twice.

**Actions:**
- Create a referral link for the one confirmed superfan. Thank them personally in a direct email. Offer them early access to whatever comes next (a paid tier, an event, a resource library).
- Build a referral mechanism into the newsletter footer for all subscribers. Use Sparkloop or a simple UTM link. Each subscriber gets a personal link that tracks their referrals. Set a visible milestone: "Refer 3 founders, get a personal 30-minute call with me."
- Identify the 5 most engaged subscribers beyond the confirmed superfan. Send each a personal email asking if they'd be willing to be featured in the newsletter (their story, a challenge they faced). This pulls them closer and generates content simultaneously.

**Metric to track:** Monthly referrals from existing subscribers. Target: 30+ subscriber referrals per month within 12 months (currently ~3/month total).

---

## Priority recommendation

Focus on Stage 2 first.

With 1,200 subscribers and a 38% open rate, the content is working. The program has enough readers to study what converts them. The highest leverage action right now is fixing the subscriber conversion rate from blog readers — a content upgrade and a rewritten opt-in prompt could triple monthly subscriber acquisition within 60 days. Once list growth accelerates, Stage 3 work (engagement) becomes easier because there are more people to engage.

Don't build a community platform yet. Five engaged email subscribers who reply regularly are more valuable than a Discord server with 200 silent members. Community infrastructure should follow organic demand, not precede it.
