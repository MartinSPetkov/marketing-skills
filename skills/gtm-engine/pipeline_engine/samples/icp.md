# ICP

Sample ICP for the pipeline_engine demo. Replace with your real target profile before running on live data.

## Who we sell to

B2B SaaS companies, roughly 50 to 500 employees, Seed to Series C.

## Target roles (buyers and champions)

- VP, Head, or Director of Marketing
- VP, Head, or Director of Growth or Demand Generation
- Head of Content
- RevOps lead or manager
- Founder or CEO at companies under ~150 people who still own marketing

## Strong-fit signals

- Owns or influences the pipeline number
- Company publishes content and cares about inbound
- Mid-market B2B SaaS, not enterprise, not pre-product

## Disqualifiers

- Students, academics, job seekers
- Solo consultants, freelancers, or agencies selling the same service
- Pure individual-contributor sales roles (SDR, AE) with no marketing remit
- Companies under ~10 people (too early) or over ~1,000 (enterprise, different motion)
- Anyone outside B2B SaaS (e-commerce, hardware, regulated pharma)
