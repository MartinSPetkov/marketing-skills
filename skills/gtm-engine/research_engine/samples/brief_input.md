# Research engine input (sample)

## Category and theme

The state of AI search visibility for B2B SaaS. How many companies are cited by AI
engines on buyer-intent queries, who is winning, and what the cited companies do
differently from those that are invisible.

## Company running the research

Catalyst (gotcatalyst.com). Catalyst runs AEO and GEO programs for B2B clients and
produces original research reports that get clients into rooms with target accounts.
The company's positioning is that content, AI visibility, and peer research form a
single revenue loop — each play feeds the next.

## ICP

B2B SaaS companies at Seed through Series C, roughly 50 to 500 employees. Target roles:
VP or Head of Marketing, VP or Head of Growth, Head of Content, and founders who still
own marketing at smaller companies. These buyers care about pipeline and measure content
on its ability to generate or accelerate it.

## Reference URLs (optional)

Add 2 to 4 public articles or existing reports on AI search, AEO, or GEO below,
one per line. The tool will fetch and synthesize them to ground the insight angle.
Leave blank and the tool will generate the angle from the category alone.
