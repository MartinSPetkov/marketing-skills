# Entity auditor: demo targets

The entity_auditor runs live on a real public site, so there is no sample data to
fabricate. It fetches the homepage, checks public sources like Wikidata, and generates
the audit at runtime.

Good demo targets:
- A real Catalyst prospect or a B2B SaaS in their world. Strongest choice: a company
  Will would recognize as a target.
- Catalyst itself (gotcatalyst.com). Bold, and it shows you came with a finding about
  their own entity footprint. Check the output first so nothing reads as a cheap shot.
- Your own past employers, which you can speak to with authority: Cyfrin and Ackee. You
  already know their gaps.

Pick a site with a clear gap (no Wikidata entry, thin Organization schema) so the before
and after lands on camera. Run it on two or three sites beforehand and demo the one with
the most visible gap.
