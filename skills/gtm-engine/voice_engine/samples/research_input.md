Finding: In a sample survey of 300 B2B marketing leaders, 68% said they have no
idea whether AI search engines mention their brand, and 4 in 5 had never checked.
Of the companies that did check, most found a competitor was named and they were not.

Angle: B2B companies are invisible in AI search and do not know it. The fix is not
more content. It is clear, specific answers to the questions buyers actually ask.
