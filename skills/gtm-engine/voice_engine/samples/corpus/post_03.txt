Everyone is building content. Almost no one is building answers.

There's a difference.

Content is what you produce to fill a calendar, rank for keywords, or demonstrate thought leadership. Answers are what buyers get when they type a specific question into an AI engine or a search bar.

Content performs for traffic. Answers perform for pipeline.

I've seen companies with 400 blog posts lose a $200k deal to a competitor with 12 pages, because those 12 pages answered the exact questions the buying committee was asking.

The volume game is over. The specificity game is just starting.
