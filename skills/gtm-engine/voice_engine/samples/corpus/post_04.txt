I spent three years at a demand gen software company telling customers their content strategy was the problem.

Then I started my own company and had the same problem.

Humbling. Also clarifying.

The gap between knowing what good content strategy looks like and actually executing it is the part nobody talks about in keynotes. You know you should write specific answers to specific buyer questions. You know you should audit your AI search visibility. You know the calendar-fill approach doesn't work.

Knowing doesn't close the gap.

The thing that finally moved us: we stopped asking "what should we write" and started asking "what question does a buyer ask the day before they book a demo."

Different question. Different answers. Different pipeline.
