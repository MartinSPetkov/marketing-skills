Four in five B2B buyers complete more than half their research before talking to a vendor.

That research now includes asking AI engines for recommendations.

What most companies miss: AI engines don't pull only from your website. They pull from every place you've published clear, specific answers. LinkedIn posts. Podcast transcripts. Documentation. Press.

Visibility is distributed. Most companies publish in one place and hope.

The companies that show up in AI recommendations are publishing the same core answers across six to eight surfaces. Not the same article repurposed. The same answer, formatted for each surface.

More work than a blog post. Less work than a lost deal.
