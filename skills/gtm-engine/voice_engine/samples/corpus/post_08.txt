The question that changed how I think about content:

"If a buyer types this into Claude tomorrow, what do they get?"

Not "what should we rank for." Not "what does our persona care about." What does a buyer actually receive when they ask a real question.

Try it. Pick the three questions your best prospects ask on discovery calls. Type each one into Claude or ChatGPT. See who answers.

If it's not you, you have a content gap, not a brand gap.

Brand gaps take years to close. Content gaps take weeks.
