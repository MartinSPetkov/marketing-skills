The first thing I check when I audit a company's GTM: do they show up when I ask Claude "what are the best tools for [their category]?"

Most don't.

The second thing: do their competitors show up?

Usually yes.

That gap is not a brand problem or a budget problem. It's a content structure problem. AI engines cite sources that answer specific questions directly. Most company content isn't structured that way.

The fix is not more content. It's a different kind of content.
