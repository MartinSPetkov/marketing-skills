I've had the wrong mental model about B2B content for most of my career.

I thought the goal was to build an audience. Subscribers, followers, shares.

The actual goal is to be the answer when a buyer asks a question. Audience is a side effect.

The companies that close the most pipeline from content are often not the ones with the biggest audiences. They're the ones that show up, clearly and specifically, when a buyer is in a decision.

That's a different optimization. Different content. Different metrics.

Worth checking which one you're actually building for.
