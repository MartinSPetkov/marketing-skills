68% of the B2B companies I've audited have no idea if AI search engines mention them.

Not "working on it." Not "not yet." Just no awareness the question exists.

Gartner puts AI-assisted browsing at 30% of sessions by 2026. Forrester puts buyer research time at 14 hours before first sales contact. Most of that research now happens in tools that don't show you the traffic.

The visibility gap is real. The problem is that most companies are optimizing for Google while buyers have already moved.

Three things I see in every audit:
1. The company's own homepage answers none of the questions buyers actually type.
2. Competitors with weaker products get named because they answer specific questions clearly.
3. The fix takes two weeks, not a year.

Most demand gen teams are still measuring impressions. The metric that matters now is whether an AI engine knows you exist and recommends you by name.

Start there.
