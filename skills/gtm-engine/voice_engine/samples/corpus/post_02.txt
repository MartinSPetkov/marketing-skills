Sales asked marketing for "better leads" 43 times last quarter.

I know because I counted. I was the CMO.

"Better leads" is the least specific ask in B2B. It means different things to every AE who says it. What they usually mean:
- The prospect already knows what category they're buying in
- They've done the research and narrowed to two or three vendors
- They're not expecting education on the first call

That's not a lead quality problem. That's a content problem.

Buyers who arrive pre-educated got there by researching on their own time. The question is whether your content shows up when they do.

Most don't.
