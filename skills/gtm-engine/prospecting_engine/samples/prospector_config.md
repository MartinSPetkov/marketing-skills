# Prospecting engine config (demo)

## Sender identity
Name: the founder
Company: Catalyst (gotcatalyst.com)
Offer: helps B2B companies get cited in AI search and turn content into pipeline.
Voice: direct, specific, no filler. See antislop_rules.md.

## Booking
Booking link: https://cal.com/catalyst/intro
Default meeting: 30 minutes, intro call.

## ICP
Target roles: VP, Head, or Director of Marketing, Growth, or Content; CMO; founder or CEO at companies under ~150 who still own marketing.
Company size: 50 to 500, B2B SaaS.
Disqualifiers: regulated healthcare or finance where messaging is constrained, companies under ~10 or over ~1,000, anyone outside B2B SaaS.

## Trigger priorities (high to low)
1. AI-search-visibility gap (our wedge; lead with it whenever present)
2. New marketing exec (reshapes the vendor stack)
3. Funding (budget plus a push to own the category)
4. Hiring a content or marketing leader (they care about the problem we solve)
5. Product launch (new category queries to win)

## Sequence (day offsets)
- Day 0: warm-up comment on the prospect's recent public post (only if one is provided)
- Day 2: connection note (under 300 characters)
- Day 3: message 1 (lead with the wedge)
- Day 6: message 2 (only if no reply)
- Day 10: email follow-up (optional channel)
A positive reply stops the sequence and books a meeting. A negative reply stops it.

## Daily caps (enforced even in dry-run)
- Comments per day: 15
- Connection requests per day: 20
- Messages per day: 30

## Scoring weights
Fit weight: 0.4
Trigger strength weight: 0.3
Wedge clarity weight: 0.3
Tiers: Hot >= 80, Warm 60 to 79, Cool below 60.
